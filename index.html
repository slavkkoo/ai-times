— Notice to Subscribers · How It Works —
1
Step the first

Copy your Kindle's address

Easier from Amazon's website than from the device itself:

  1. Open Amazon's Preferences — Content & Devices and sign in if asked.
  2. Scroll to Personal Document Settings.
  3. Under Send-to-Kindle E-Mail Settings, find your Kindle and copy its address (yourname@kindle.com).
2
Step the second

Let the paper through the door

  1. On the same page, scroll to Approved Personal Document E-mail List.
  2. Select Add a new approved e-mail address.
  3. Enter aitimes.paper@gmail.com and click Add Address.
  4. Paste your Kindle email into the coupon below.

Skip this step and Amazon turns the paper away at the door — silently.
